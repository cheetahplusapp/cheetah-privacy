Cheetah+ - Account Deletion & Privacy Policy

How to Delete Your Account

If you would like to delete your Cheetah+ account and associated data, you can do so directly from the app:

  1. Open the Cheetah+ app and log in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap on Delete Account and confirm your request.

Alternatively, you can request account and data deletion by sending an email to our support team. We will process and delete your data within 48 hours.

Data We Keep or Delete

When an account deletion request is processed, all personal profile data, ride/delivery history, and credentials are permanently removed from our active databases, except where retention is required by law.
